Many only remember the TEL as a means to get to RP, but it also connects RP with the rest of Singapore at a tap of your ez-link card. The line is home to many other MRT stations that can make your after school trip a little more exciting with friends.

Well, what are you waiting for? Miss your usual stop, and explore a world beyond your daily commute.
